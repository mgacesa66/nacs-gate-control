# NaCs molecular-gate publication plan — frozen v1.0

**Frozen:** 2026-11-02, upon research-project approval. This document records the agreed sequence; its wording and original task boundaries are not retroactively changed. Amendments must be separately versioned and dated.

**Scientific destination:** a reproducible arXiv preprint focused on physically constrained complete-operation fidelity, multilevel spectator leakage, basis selection and spectator-redistribution counterexamples; followed by a potential PRA submission. Not a claim of a measured high-fidelity gate.

1. **Molecular-parameter sensitivity with carrier recalibration.** Distinguish frozen-carrier drift from frequency tracking, phase calibration and microwave-amplitude recalibration. Evaluate model changes in B field, effective optical tensor and ellipticity, and test whether leakage-redistribution survives them. Record eigenstate-label ambiguity and explicitly hypothetical parameter ranges. **Current work.**
2. **Systematic molecular basis enlargement.** Rank all 128 molecular states by couplings/detunings across the actual pulse spectrum; propagate successively larger pair bases and verify both the channel and relative control ranking, going beyond 11/13/15 states.
3. **Matched control-objective comparison.** Reoptimize isolated transfer, one-spectator suppression and complete-channel fidelity under equal control resources, power, phase bandwidth, duration and optimization effort. Report held-out control noise and detailed leakage, no deprecated nine-state headline claims.
4. **Literature and novelty audit.** Carefully position the contribution relative to Picard et al., Bergonzoni/Jandura/Pupillo, Lu/Shi and related spectroscopy/optimal-control literature.
5. **Write and illustrate the manuscript.** Approximately 10–12 pages plus numerical appendices; make original, reproducible figures tied to release data and clearly separate predicted and measured observables.
6. **Freeze the paper results and submit.** Tag the complete source and datasets, cite version/checksums, prepare arXiv-compliant LaTeX source and check category/endorsement requirements.

**Release policy:** Preserve the public GitHub v0.1.0 archive unmodified; this working extension is a separate version. Do not promote numerical selected-state convergence to experimental validity. If Step 1 reveals severe model dependence, narrow claims before proceeding. No promise that all six steps have been completed.
